# Play Store listing: Pune Transit Guide

## App name (30 chars max)
Pune Transit Guide

## Short description (80 chars max)
Plan Pune metro journeys with routes, stations, timetables and fare information.

## Full description (plain text, paste as-is into Play Console)

```text
Independent Pune travel and metro guide

Plan a metro trip in Pune. Look up metro routes, the metro stations on each line, a city map, the published train timetable and metro fares.

Key features

Route planner. Choose two stations and see the line, the stations in order, and an estimated journey time. The time is spread from the line's published end-to-end duration. It is not a live arrival.

Another route, when one exists. If there is a second way to travel, the app can show it.

City map. Follow one line at a time, with numbered stops and station details.

Nearby stations. When you are in Pune, the app can list the closest stations from your location. This stays hidden when you are more than 100 km from Pune.

Train timetable. See the published first train, last train, and how often trains run. This is not a departure time for every station.

Metro fares. A fare is shown only when a verified fare is stored. If none is available, the app says so instead of guessing a price.

Saved on your phone. After the metro data has loaded once, you can look it up again without a connection. The first open needs the internet.

Light and dark theme. The app opens in the light theme. You can switch it on the home screen.

Why use it

- Simple screens for planning, lines, the map, and the timetable
- Covers Pune Metro lines, stations and published timings in one place
- No made-up fares, live trains, or station-by-station departure board

Notes

The map is for information only. Station positions and the drawn line can be incomplete, and the app is not responsible for a wrong pin or a wrong turn. Always check official sources before you travel.
```
